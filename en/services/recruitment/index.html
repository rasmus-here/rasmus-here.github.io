Recruitment

We help organisations define their needs, assess candidates and recruit digital talent that fits both the role and the team.

How we help

Find the right digital talent with support from specialists who understand the roles in depth.

We always start by understanding the current situation, the business goals and what obstacles stand in the way of the next step. After that, we prioritize activities that can be measured, implemented and followed up together with the right people in your organization.

  • Capability needs and role profile
  • Interview support and skills assessment
  • Advice for selection and onboarding

The right role starts with the right need

Digital roles often span several disciplines. We help you distinguish what the organisation needs now, what can be developed internally and which capabilities genuinely need to be recruited.

A role profile that reflects the day-to-day work

We translate goals, responsibilities and ways of working into a clear role profile with realistic expectations for experience, specialist knowledge and collaboration.

More confident candidate assessment

We support you with interview questions, work samples and specialist assessment, creating a stronger basis for decisions and reducing the risk of a poor hire.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about recruitment

Which digital roles can you help us recruit?

We support recruitment in digital marketing, advertising, SEO, analytics, CRM, content and digital strategy, among other areas.

Can you help even if we run the recruitment process ourselves?

Yes. We can limit the support to needs analysis, role profiling, interviews, work samples or assessment of final candidates.

How do you assess specialist expertise?

We use the role's actual responsibilities and relevant questions, cases or work samples to assess both methodological knowledge and practical problem-solving.

Can you help with onboarding?

Yes. We can propose a practical plan for the first months, including goals, priorities, tools and knowledge transfer.
