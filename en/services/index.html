FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which channels are you operational in?

We work with SEM, SEO and GEO, paid social, data collection, reporting, lead management, training and business development from a marketing perspective.

How do you work with SEO?

We start with technical conditions, content, link image and competitors. We then draw up an action plan for better positions, more organic traffic and greater business benefit.

How do you work with tracking and data collection?

We implement tracking via Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and advertising tools such as Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads and LinkedIn Ads.

Can you deliver a comprehensive solution?

Yes. We have a developed network of specialized consultants and agencies in everything from content and design to system development and integrations.
