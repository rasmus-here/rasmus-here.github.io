Premier Partner with close access to Google

HERE is a Google Premier Partner, placing us in the top tier of agencies working with Google Ads. It gives us access to news, training, beta features, strategic insights and in some cases customized reports from Google themselves.

Certified specialists

All of our Google Ads specialists are certified within Google's training courses. This means that accounts, campaigns and budgets are managed by people who continuously stay up to date on Search, Performance Max, Shopping, YouTube, measurement and automation.

Account structure that scales

We build Google Ads accounts with clear structure for campaigns, goals, search terms, budget and measurement. It makes it easier to understand what is working and where the budget should be moved.

From clicks to business results

Optimization is not just about CPC and CTR. We link advertising to margins, conversion rates, lead quality and sales data whenever possible, so that decisions are more business-oriented.

Ongoing optimization and testing

We work with keyword analysis, ad texts, landing pages, negative keywords, target groups and bid strategies. Each test should contribute to better understanding and stronger results over time.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Google Ads

Is HERE a Google Premier Partner?

Yes. HERE is a Google Premier Partner and thus has access to the latest in Google Ads, such as training, beta functions, platform insights and support from Google.

Are your Google Ads specialists certified?

Yes. All of our Google Ads specialists are certified in Google's training courses and are continuously updated on changes in the platform.

Can Google contribute with customized reports?

As a Premier Partner, we can in certain situations gain access to in-depth insights, reports and recommendations from Google to support strategic decisions.

Can you improve existing Google Ads accounts?

Yes. We often start with a review of structure, tracking, search terms, budget and results before proposing new priorities.

Do you work with e-commerce and B2B?

Yes. We work both with e-commerce, lead generation and B2B where Google Ads are linked to the respective business goals.

How much does it cost to get help with Google Ads?

The fee depends on account size, number of markets and how much operational responsibility we take. In addition to the media budget for Google, we propose a transparent model for our work after an initial review.
