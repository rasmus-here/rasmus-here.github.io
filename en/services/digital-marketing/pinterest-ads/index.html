Advertising where the customer plans

Pinterest is often used when people plan purchases, projects, interior design, lifestyle, fashion, food, travel and other visual decisions. This makes the channel interesting for brands that want to influence before the customer has made up his mind.

Visual material with clear function

We help dictate and structure creative formats for Pinterest. The goal is for the content to be inspiring but also clearly linked to the product, category, offer or landing page.

Measurement and channel role

Pinterest needs to be followed up based on its role in the customer journey. We work with pixels, UTMs, directories and reporting to understand how the channel contributes to traffic, engagement, conversions and assistive effect.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Pinterest Ads

When are Pinterest Ads appropriate?

Pinterest is particularly suitable for visual products, inspiration, e-commerce and categories where the customer plans or compares before buying.

Can Pinterest Ads Drive Sales?

Yes, especially when campaigns are linked to the right product feed, landing pages, pixel and clear creative strategy.

Do you help with Pinterest creative?

Yes. We can set requirements for format, message, visual direction and test structure together with creators or the customer's internal team.

How does a startup with Pinterest Ads work?

We connect product feed or catalog, set up pixel and UTM, set requirements for visual material and build campaigns based on where in the planning phase the target group is.

How do you measure the results of Pinterest Ads?

We measure traffic, conversions and assisted impact using the Pinterest tag, UTM parameters and catalogue data, because Pinterest often contributes early in a visual buying journey.
