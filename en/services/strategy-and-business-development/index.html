Strategy & Business Development

We help you connect business goals, market position, offer and digital investments so that the work becomes focused, measurable and possible to implement.

How we help

Maximize returns with the right strategies, priorities and resources.

We always start by understanding the current situation, the business goals and what obstacles stand in the way of the next step. After that, we prioritize activities that can be measured, implemented and followed up together with the right people in your organization.

  • Current situation analysis and growth plan
  • Go-to-market and channel strategy
  • Priorities, budget and roadmap

Strategy that can be used

A digital strategy needs to be more than a presentation. We help companies translate goals, current situation and market situation into concrete priorities, budget decisions and activities that can be run in everyday life.

Business development with a digital edge

We analyze the offer, target group, buying journey, competition, data and organization to find where growth can actually be created. It could be about new channels, better packaging, more efficient lead flows or a clearer market position.

Roadmap and governance

When the direction is set, we build a roadmap with responsibility, pace, measurement points and decision-making basis. The goal is to create a strategy that makes it easier to prioritize, follow up and say no to things that don't drive the business forward.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about strategy & business development

When do you need help with digital strategy?

When investments in marketing, sales, data or technology need to be linked more clearly to business goals, growth and profitability.

What does a growth plan include?

A growth plan contains current situation analysis, goals, prioritized target groups, channel strategy, budget recommendation, measurement points and a concrete roadmap.

Can HERE support management teams?

Yes. We often work with management, market and sales to create consensus around digital investments, expectations and follow-up.

How long does strategy work take?

A focused current situation analysis and prioritization often takes a few weeks, while a full growth and go-to-market plan can take longer. We deliver concrete decision-making materials on an ongoing basis so that you can start acting before the entire work is finished.

What does a digital strategy cost?

The price depends on the scope, number of markets and how deep the analysis needs to be. After an initial meeting, we propose a defined delivery with a clear purpose, format and price.
