Robin Pettersson

Digital Growth Specialist

Robin works with sales, digital growth and education. He combines business perspective, communication and operational experience in digital marketing.

Experience that creates value for HERE's customers

Robin has been involved since the start of HERE. Before that, he worked together with Niklas, Alex and Gustav at the agency Teveo.

Robin works closely with companies that want to create a clearer connection between marketing, sales and business goals. As a trainer, he also shares practical knowledge about digital marketing and how the channels contribute to the business.

He also works with AI-assisted system development where HERE codes websites, digital services and internal tools for both customers and its own operations.

His profile combines communication with solid experience in Google Ads. Robin has completed Google's training in Search and Display and works with both strategic path choices and concrete implementation.
