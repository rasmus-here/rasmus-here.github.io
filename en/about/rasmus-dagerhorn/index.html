Experience that creates value for HERE's customers

Rasmus describes his professional orientation as digital growth and works to make digital channels more understandable, measurable and useful for customers. He has particular expertise in tracking and practical application of GDPR in digital marketing, with a focus on creating reliable and privacy-aware measurement.

He also works with AI-assisted system development of websites, digital services and tools for both internal needs and customer assignments.

As an educational consultant, he teaches, among other things, the basics of Google Ads, ad copy, display advertising and Shopify. The combination of customer work and teaching means that he continuously translates platform changes into practical methods.
