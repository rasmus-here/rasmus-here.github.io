FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Feed validator

What feed formats are supported?

The tool supports CSV, TSV and XML feeds in RSS 2.0 format with Google's g: attribute.

Is the product feed uploaded?

No. All validation takes place locally in your browser. Product data is not sent to HERE or Merchant Center.

What does the feed validator check?

The tool checks for required attributes, price formats, inventory balance, duplicate product IDs, image links, product identifiers, and GTIN check digits, among other things. It also shows alerts and data coverage.
