FAQ

Frequently asked questions about UTM builder with naming convention

What are UTM parameters?

UTM parameters are tags added to a URL to describe campaign, source, medium and content. They make it possible to follow traffic and results in, for example, Google Analytics 4.

Why is a naming convention important?

A common naming convention prevents the same channel from being written in several different ways. It provides cleaner reports, fewer Unassigned sessions and comparable data over time.

Is the campaign link saved at HERE?

No. The link is created locally in your browser and is not sent to HERE or any other server.
