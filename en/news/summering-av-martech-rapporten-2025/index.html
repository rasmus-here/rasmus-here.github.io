A Swedish summary of the "Martech Report 2025"

Now a new version of the Martech report is out. Here you can read our Swedish summary.

Of Rasmus Dagerhorn · Peer reviewed 5 August 2026

In this article

The martech landscape continues to growData and integrations become crucialAI makes work faster, but not automatically betterOur view on developmentThree questions to ask before your next martech investment

Short summary

The most important thing to take with you

  • More tools do not automatically create a better martech stack.
  • Integrations, data quality and clear ownership become more important as AI increases its pace.
  • Proprietary solutions are becoming more available, but still need requirements, security and management.
01

The martech landscape continues to grow

Martech Report 2025 shows how quickly tools, platforms and AI support continue to change the everyday life of marketing departments. For many companies, the challenge is no longer to find a tool, but to choose the right combination and get the systems to talk to each other.

02

Data and integrations become crucial

When more tools are used in parallel, the demands on tracking, data quality and clear processes increase. Without a well-thought-out data foundation, teams risk spending time on reporting that cannot be trusted.

03

AI makes work faster, but not automatically better

AI can give marketers more power in analysis, content and development, but the effect will only come when the technology is linked to clear goals, good data and human prioritization.

04

Our view on development

For companies that want to grow digitally, martech becomes a business issue rather than a pure system issue. The right setup should make it easier to make decisions, optimize budgets and create better experiences for customers.

05

Three questions to ask before your next martech investment

Start by asking which decision or workflow the tool should improve, who owns the implementation and how data should flow to other systems. If the answers are missing, there is a high risk that the investment will create yet another isolated data source. A smaller stack with clear owners and well-functioning integrations often provides more impact than many overlapping platforms.
