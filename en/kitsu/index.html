The starting point

Marketing work is scattered across far too many tools

Forms in one system, leads in another, reports in a third and the planning in a spreadsheet. Nobody has the full picture, and time goes into moving information between tabs instead of improving results. Kitsu is our answer to that.

What you get

One portal, your modules

Only what you need

Kitsu is modular. You switch on the parts that are relevant to you, and new modules can be added without touching what already works.

Everything is connected

A lead from a quiz lands in the CRM. Ad data appears in the dashboard. The meeting you just had becomes a transcript in the right client folder. No exports, no manual wiring.

Built for multiple projects

The portal handles several clients and projects in parallel, in Swedish and English, with clear roles and permissions for everyone who needs access.

AI in Kitsu

An AI that is allowed to do things, not just answer

In most platforms the AI is a chat box next to the product. In Kitsu it has the same rights you do in every module. Ask it to build a form, fill in the activity plan or rewrite a quiz result, and it actually happens.

Kitsu always works within your client and project context. It sees your data and your modules, nobody else's.

Data and security

Your data, inside the EU, separated from everyone else's

  • Dedicated database schemas per client and module – not just a client id column in a shared table.
  • Three layers of access control – schema grants, security rules in the database and checks in the application.
  • Roles and permissions per client and project, so everyone sees exactly what they should.
  • Logging of administrative actions and encrypted storage of integration keys.
  • Data is stored within the EU.

Because we build the platform ourselves, we can tell you exactly where your data sits and who can reach it, without passing you on to a vendor.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Kitsu

What is Kitsu?

Kitsu is a marketing portal built by HERE. Instead of spreading the work across a row of separate tools, you get one workspace where content, forms, leads, planning, ad data and analysis are connected – with an AI assistant that can carry out the work inside the modules.

Who can use Kitsu?

Kitsu is available to every client we work with. The portal suits both the small marketing function that wants everything in one place and the larger company that needs several projects and clear permission control.

Do we have to use every module?

No. Kitsu is modular and you only switch on what is relevant. More modules can be added later without affecting what you already use.

Can you build a module we are missing?

Yes. We build new modules continuously, both when clients ask for something specific and when we see a recurring need ourselves. Because we own the platform, we can build and roll it out without waiting for an external vendor.

What can the AI in Kitsu do?

Kitsu's AI can genuinely create and change things inside the modules: build a form, add questions to a quiz, fill in the activity plan or rewrite copy. It always works within your client and project context and only sees your data.

Which data sources can be connected?

Kitsu pulls data from GA4, Google Ads, Meta Ads and TikTok Ads. On top of that there are integrations with Shopify for product feeds, Google Drive for archiving and Looker Studio for reports.

Where is our data stored?

Your data is stored within the EU. Each client also gets dedicated database schemas per module, and access is protected in three layers: schema grants, security rules in the database and checks in the application.

What does Kitsu cost?

The setup depends on which modules you use and how we work together in general. Get in touch and we will go through what makes sense for your situation.
